Sort & Delete - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Short version: Sort & Delete collects nothing. Everything stays on your device.

What the app does

Sort & Delete helps you review the photos and videos on your device: keep them, move them to folders, or delete them via your device's trash. All of this happens locally on your phone.

Data collection

Sort & Delete does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no internet access - it does not even request the network permission - so nothing can leave your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDKs.

Photo and video access

The app requests access to your photo and video library because reviewing, organizing and deleting your media is its core (and only) function. Your media and your review decisions are processed and stored only on your device. Review progress is kept in a small local database inside the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Deletion

Photos you delete are moved to your device's system trash (Android 11+) where the system removes them permanently after about 30 days, unless you restore them earlier.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
